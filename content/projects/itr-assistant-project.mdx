## Story Time: Filing Crypto Taxes with CoinDCX

I got a call from my CA asking for the **PnL statement** for my crypto account with **CoinDCX**. I thought it'd take **5–10 minutes** to get it from the app and send the report.  

It took me **4 days**.  

Not only was my precious time wasted, this all happened during the **Ganpati festival**, so I had to miss one of the rituals just to file taxes with my CA.  


![Sneak Peek](/images/projects/itr-assistant/1pic.png)


### What was the Problem?

CoinDCX doesn't provide the PnL statement right away.  
Instead, they redirect you to **KoinX**, which is a **paid service**, essentially forcing you into a subscription.  

Basically, they want to **squeez in money for something that should be free**.  

They do have a trade ledger, so if you want, you can manually decode the PnL from that.  



### The Instant Order Ledger (10 Columns):
| Trade ID | Crypto | Trade Completion Time | Side | Avg Price (INR) | Quantity | Gross (INR) | Fees (INR) | Net (INR) | TDS (INR) |
|:--------:|:------:|:---------------------:|:----:|----------------:|---------:|------------:|-----------:|----------:|----------:|

My *engineer gene* kicked in.  
“How hard can it be?” I thought, and started making my own PnL sheet.  

Turns out, it's hard. It's not exactly *hard-hard*, but **tedious-hard**.  


### CA’s Format

My CA sent me a format of the column he wanted.  

| Particulars | Date of Purchase | Date of Transfer | Sale Consideration | Cost of Acquisition | Income (loss ignored) |
|-------------|------------------|------------------|--------------------|---------------------|-----------------------|


I copy-pasted the values (without averaging or using any proper technique) and sent them to him.  
- He gave a review (but didn’t mention **FIFO method**).  
- I updated the sheet and sent it again.  

This time I used the **Averaging Method** (average the price of all the shares bought before the sell trade).  

This process took **2 days**.  

But… he was still unsatisfied.  

After some back and forth, he mentioned the **FIFO method**.  

> 💡 **Fact Box: FIFO Method**  
>   
> Let’s say you have 3 trades:  
> 1. **Buy** → 100 shares → ₹10/share  
> 2. **Buy** → 200 shares → ₹20/share  
> 3. **Sell** → 150 shares → ₹15/share  
>   
> When the 3rd trade is executed:  
> - First, the 100 shares of Order 1 are sold  
> - Then, 50 shares from Order 2  
>   
> **Profit calculation:**  
> ```
> 100 * (15 - 10) + 50 * (15 - 20) = 250
> ```
>   
> Profit = **₹250**

The result was satisfactory. It didn't take much brains to implement the method but it was tedious and a long process. 

### Lessons Learned

After going through all this, it was clear:  
- This is just a **computation task**.  
- No AI needed, only some **logic + basics**.  


### Building an App to Automate It 

To make my life easier for the next quarter, I built an **App**.  

- **Backend:** I implemented the logic to parse CoinDCX’s *instant trades* Excel file.  
- **Frontend:** Used BOLT.new to quickly design the UI.  

🔗 [Check it out here](https://coineddcx-itr-assistant.netlify.app/)
, you can also try pasting https://coineddcx-itr-assistant.netlify.app/ in yourt browser. 


> ### Backend Explanation
>
> The CoinDCX instant trade Excel file had **2 sheets**.  
> - Sheet 2 (`instant trades`) was the one required.  
> 
> Steps I followed:  
> 1. Identify the **Trade ID** in the first column.  
> 2. Select the next 10 columns for operations.  
> 3. Group trades by crypto symbols.  
> 4. Pass these grouped trades to `processExcelData`.  
> 
> Inside `processExcelData`:  
> - Split the trades into **Buy** and **Sell** orders.  
> - Use the Sell orders to deduct the number of tokens from the Buy orders accordingly.  
>
