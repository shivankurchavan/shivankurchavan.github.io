## The problem

The system was processing a significant number of transactions and P99 latency was reaching approximately 400–500ms.

The challenge was understanding whether the issue came from queries, contention, infrastructure or workload behavior.

## Investigation

We started by collecting metrics and identifying patterns rather than immediately changing queries.

This helped narrow the search space.

## Changes

Several smaller changes were introduced and the results were observed over time.

Interestingly, the improvement wasn't immediate.

Latency gradually improved over several hours.

## Result

The final system showed significantly better tail latency while maintaining throughput.

## What I learned

Performance work is often less about finding a single magic optimization and more about systematically understanding the system.
