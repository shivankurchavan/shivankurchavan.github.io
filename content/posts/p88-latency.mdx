The system was touching nearly 76,000 transactions.

Initially, P99 latency was hovering around 400–500ms.

The interesting part was that after a few changes, latency didn't immediately improve.

Instead, it gradually fell over several hours.

## The problem

The first question wasn't:

> How do we make the database faster?

It was:

> What is actually causing the latency?

That distinction changed how we investigated the problem.

## What we found

The bottleneck wasn't a single slow query.

It was a combination of factors that only became visible once we started looking at the right metrics.

## What I learned

Most performance problems are observability problems first.

You can't optimize what you don't understand.
